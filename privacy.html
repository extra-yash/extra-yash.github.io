Who we are

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts and some images load from Google Fonts and Framer. Those providers receive your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches their files, as described in their own privacy policies.

Our business emails

We email companies that have publicly advertised a design role, to offer our design services. For that we hold the company's name, the job listing, and the work email address the company itself published on the listing, its careers page or its website, with the name and job title shown next to it where there is one. We also keep the emails we exchange. We never buy lists, guess addresses or test mail servers for them.

Our basis is our legitimate interest in offering design services to businesses that are hiring for design. If you do not answer, we delete your details after 180 days. If you opt out, we delete them and keep only a one-way hash of your address and domain, so that we never contact you again.

To opt out, reply "no" to any of our emails or use the unsubscribe link at its foot. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, at any time by writing to hello@extracollective.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Office of Mauritius.

Extra Outreach and Google user data

Extra Outreach is our internal tool for those emails. It is used only by Extra's own Google account and is not offered to anyone else. With our permission it uses the Gmail API to:

  • send our emails from our own mailbox as hello@extracollective.com;
  • read new messages in our mailbox to find replies to those emails, so each conversation stays together. Messages that are not replies to our emails are not stored;
  • read our Gmail "send mail as" settings, to check that hello@extracollective.com is set up to send.

Data from Gmail is stored only on our own computer in Mauritius and is read only by Extra's team. We do not sell it, share it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine learning models. Extra Outreach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
