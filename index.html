03 / WHY EXTRA

The Future of
Creative Work is
not Where You Are.

In 2020, the studios went dark.

The spaces, equipment and infrastructure we thought we needed disappeared overnight. The work did not. It became more immediate, more resourceful and more essential.

Then came international work across cities and time zones: complex briefs for UN agencies, distributed teams and real stakes.

There was no shared room; only clear intent, responsibility and people who knew why they were there.

EXTRA WAS BUILT ON THAT EVIDENCE: GEOGRAPHY IS A DETAIL. A PHYSICAL STRUCTURE IS A HABIT, NOT A REQUIREMENT.

Extra is what happens when the right people stop waiting for the right conditions.
